# /lp/joint-knee-pain — what changed, and why

For Dr. Baumgartner's review. This is the only one of the seven ad landing
pages that needed copy rewritten rather than selected, because PRP carried a
large share of the clinical argument on both `/joint-pain` and `/knee-pain`.

The live pages at `/joint-pain` and `/knee-pain` are **unchanged**. This log
covers only the new page at `/lp/joint-knee-pain`.

Google Ads disapproved the practice's ads under its policy on speculative and
experimental medical treatments, which covers PRP, stem cells and regenerative
therapies. The landing pages may not mention that class of treatment anywhere —
body copy, title, meta description, image alt text, or structured data.

Everything below is drawn from copy the site already publishes. No treatment was
invented for this page.

---

## 3. Recovery & outlook

| | |
|---|---|
| **Was** | Both pages described PRP's slower biological timeline alongside the ablation and injection timelines, plus "NSAIDs stopped for 7 days beforehand." |
| **Now** | Keeps the ablation timeline verbatim (about 15 minutes, light activity next day, full benefit near the 6-week mark, commonly 6–18 months, repeatable) and the faster-but-shorter steroid / hyaluronic acid timeline. ## 4. FAQ — two removed, two rewritten

**Removed entirely** (both were only about PRP):

- "Is PRP right for my joint?"
- "Is PRP covered by insurance?" and "How many PRP injections will I need?"

**Rewritten:**

| Question | Change |
|---|---|
| "Can anything help knee arthritis short of replacement?" | Dropped the PRP clause. Image-guided injections, hyaluronic acid and genicular ablation now carry the answer. The closing line — that replacement is the better answer for severe end-stage arthritis — is kept. |
| "How often can I have a steroid injection?" | Was: repeated steroid can degrade tissue, "which is one reason we prefer biologics for tendon problems." Now: keeps the three-to-four-per-region-per-year limit and the reason, and points to a durable option such as nerve ablation when injections are needed every few months. |
| "Will a steroid injection damage my knee?" | Merged into the question above rather than kept separately, for the same reason — its answer also ended on preferring biologics. |

**Kept unchanged:** "What is genicular nerve ablation?", "Is my joint pain
actually coming from my back?", "Which joints do you treat?", "I still have pain
after my knee replacement. Is that treatable?", "Do I need a driver for a knee
procedure?"

## 6. Scope and links

- One page covering knee, hip and shoulder, instead of two cross-linked pages.
- **No outbound links at all** except the HTx logo (to the homepage) and the

  menu, which is what triggered the disapproval.
